Cumulative net cash: −C₀ at start, then + (benefit − Cᵣ) per year. Other lines use your benefit and C₀ with the reference costs of the neighbouring options.

Formulas

Benefit = tasks/day × days × minutes/60 × cost/h × r
ROI after n years = (n × Benefit − C₀ − n × Cᵣ) / (C₀ + n × Cᵣ)
No discounting. Break-even = C₀ / monthly net benefit.

Read critically

Values illustrate structure, not your result. The minutes saved and r are the most uncertain inputs: measure them in a pilot. Prices and regulatory timelines change often. Continuum positions after Srivastava, Govindarajan & Enache (2026), California Management Review 68(4).
